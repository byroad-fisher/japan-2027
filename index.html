JDM car culture

Keep one Tokyo night on a Friday or Saturday. The plan separates three very different things: street-karting for the Mario Kart-style experience, a JDM/car-meet evening, and actual circuit drifting.

Daikoku PA

Iconic modified-car gathering point, but it sits inside the expressway system. It is not a train-access attraction and gatherings are informal, so we'll arrange suitable transport rather than promise a particular meet.

If actual drifting is the priority, we'll check 2027 circuit/event calendars before booking and can swap a Tokyo activity for it.

RAIL STRATEGY

Don't activate the pass on arrival.

Current online price: ¥50,000 for a 7-day ordinary Japan Rail Pass. The working plan activates it on Day 4, Tokyo → Kyoto, then uses its final days for the expensive intercity legs back to Tokyo.

Before purchase we'll re-price every journey against individual tickets. JR Pass prices and rules can change before 2027. Nozomi/Mizuho currently need an additional special ticket.

JUNE REALITY CHECK

Warm, humid,
possibly wet.

June is generally rainy-season territory in much of Japan, but that does not mean rain every day. Tokyo's long-term June averages are around 26°C high / 18°C low. The itinerary deliberately keeps flexible indoor options.
